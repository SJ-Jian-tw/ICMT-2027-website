AUTHORS INFORMATION

CONFERENCE TOPICS

Select a topic to see its sub-topics.

  • Advanced Semiconductor Processes and Packaging

    Advanced Process Nodes and EUV Lithography, Process Monitoring and Control, Advanced Packaging (2.5D/3D Integration), Chiplet and Heterogeneous Integration, Wide-Bandgap Semiconductors (SiC, GaN), Smart Fab and Equipment Automation

  • Advanced Mechatronics Devices, Sensing and Control, and Control Systems

    Robotics, Mobile and Factory Robots, Motion and Precision Control, Intelligent and Adaptive Systems, Parallel Mechanisms, Man-Machine Interfaces, Sensors, Computer Vision and Recognition, Automated Navigation, Modeling, Tele-operation, Intelligent and Advanced Control

  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Optimisation in Mechatronics

    Data/AI-driven Modelling and Control of Mechatronic Systems, Machine Learning and Deep Learning for Diagnostics, Prognostics, Predictive Maintenance; Game-theoretic and Algorithmic Optimisation and Decision-Making for Robotics and Automation.

  • Smart Actuators and Materials

    Actuators using Functional Fluid, SMA Actuators, Piezo-Electric Actuators, Electro-Static Actuators, Magneto-Strictive Actuators, Smart Materials and Structures: Ionic Polymer–Metal Composites, Dielectric Electro Active Polymer

  • MEMS/NEMS and Micro/Nano-Manufacturing

    Precision Mechatronics, Micro/Nano-Electro Mechanical Systems, Opto-Mechatronics, Ultra-High Precision Machining, Focus Ion Beam Manufacturing

  • Precision Measuring Technology

    Dimensional Measurement, Micro/Nano Measurement, Ultra High Precision Positioning, Optical and Laser Technology, Scanning Probe Microscopy Technology, Measurement for Extreme Environments, Precision Instrumentation, Metrology

  • Production Systems

    Automation Science and Technology, Intelligent Machine Tools, Industry 4.0, Flexible and Agile Manufacturing, Production Planning and Control, Fault Monitoring and Diagnosis, e-Maintenance, e-Manufacturing, Rapid Prototyping, and Remote Manufacturing

  • Sustainable Transport and Energy Systems

    Automotive Hybridization and Electrification, Vehicle Lightweight Materials and Structures, High Power Energy Storage Systems, Advanced Propulsion and Energy Saving Technologies, System Modelling and Real-Time Estimation, Energy Management Strategies and Optimization, Conditional Monitoring Systems

  • Mechatronics as an Enabler for Innovative and Intelligent Transportation Systems

    Telematics, Big Data Mining, Deep Learning, Smart, Connected and Autonomous Vehicles

  • Renewable Energy and Smart Grid

    Sustainable Energy Resources, Energy Conversion Devices, Control Technologies, Energy Generation

  • Bioengineering and Mechatronics Applications in Life Sciences

    Biosensors, Biochip Manipulation, Medical equipment, Human-Friendly Mechatronics, Human Adaptive Mechatronics, Life Cycle Design, Recycle Technology, Ecological Design, Production

  • Information and Networking

    Network-Based Control Systems, Real-Time Systems, Embedded Systems, Self-Configuring Wireless Sensor Networks

  • Human Resource Development and Education on Mechatronics Technology

    Mechatronics education, virtual reality applications, and policy strategies

  • Internet in Industry 4.0

    Internet of Things, Internet based Manufacturing, AI, Super Smart Society

  • Composite and Polymer Materials

    Advanced Composites for Smart Devices, Polymers for Lightweight Structures in Mechatronic and Automation Systems, and Smart Integration of Polymers in Actuators, Sensors, and Manufacturing Technologies
