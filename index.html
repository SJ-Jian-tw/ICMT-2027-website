ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

Marking its 30th edition, the International Conference on Mechatronics Technology (ICMT 2027) will take place in Taipei, Taiwan, hosted by National Taipei University of Technology. ICMT has long served as an international meeting point where researchers, engineers, and industry practitioners share recent advances in mechatronics. Building on this tradition, ICMT 2027 will connect academia and industry around emerging technologies and their real-world applications. The program will feature keynote speeches, oral sessions, and poster presentations across a broad spectrum of mechatronics topics.
